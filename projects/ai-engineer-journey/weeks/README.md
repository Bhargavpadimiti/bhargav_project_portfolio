# Weekly guides

Each week has a day-by-day guide here (`week-NN.md`). Your tutor writes the next week's
guide, quiz and exercises during the Sunday check-in, adapted to how the week went.
The full-programme outline is in [curriculum/plan.yaml](../curriculum/plan.yaml).

## The weekly loop

1. **Monday:** `journey status` and read this week's guide.
2. **During the week:** study, then `journey log <hours> "what you did" -t depth|build|career|review` after every session.
3. **Exercises:** edit `exercises/week-NN/weekNN.py` until `journey check week-NN` passes.
4. **Sunday:**
   - `journey quiz week-NN` (retake after reviewing if under 80%)
   - Write `journal/week-NN.md` from [the template](../journal/TEMPLATE.md)
   - `journey report`, then commit on a branch and open a pull request
   - **Check-in with the tutor:** review the week, then get next week's materials

## Falling behind (it will happen)

`journey status` says when you're more than 2 hours behind the expected pace. Then:

1. **Drop stretch items first** (marked STRETCH), never the edge-case tests.
2. **Keep the minimum:** exercises plus quiz plus the week's project milestone.
3. **Use the buffer weeks:** week 4 (holidays), week 5 (catch-up), week 13 and week 26 (checkpoints) have slack.
4. **More than a week behind?** Ask the tutor to re-plan. Dates move; standards don't.
5. **Ill, travelling or busy at work?** Log a 0-hour week in your journal and resume. Consistency over months beats intensity over days.
